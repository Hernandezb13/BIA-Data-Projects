# 🎧 Fresh Beats BI Report-Based Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This report-based business intelligence project was completed as part of the **TripleTen Business Intelligence Program**. As a junior BI analyst, I was tasked with evaluating business performance and user behavior for **Fresh Beats**, an online music streaming platform that supports rising artists while monetizing through artist promotions and paid subscriptions.

The report analyzes user engagement, subscription behavior, and genre trends to provide actionable recommendations that align with business needs.

## 🏢 Company: Fresh Beats

Fresh Beats is an innovative platform combining music streaming for listeners with promotional opportunities for emerging artists. The company earns revenue by helping musicians gain exposure and converting users to paid tiers.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenges

- **Artist Promotion Effectiveness**: Ensure musicians receive maximum exposure and ROI through platform promotions.
- **User Engagement and Conversion**: Increase user retention and convert free users into paying subscribers.
- **Genre Promotion Strategy**: Optimize content recommendation systems to promote emerging artists in trending genres.

## 📈 Status Summary

Fresh Beats has seen success in promoting new musicians but needs refinement in three core areas:

- Improve **promotion strategy performance** by identifying what drives high-impact campaigns.
- Increase **conversion rates** by understanding user behaviors that lead to subscription.
- Enhance **genre targeting** for emerging artists to align platform recommendations with music trends and user preferences.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Visuals

### 📊 Genre Trends
**Insights:**
- Pop peaked in 2021 and is now declining.
- Electronic and hip hop are trending upward.

**Recommendations:**
- Focus marketing efforts on fans of electronic and hip hop.
- Investigate the decline in pop’s popularity.
- Analyze retention within high-growth genres.

**Visual A:** (Genre Trends Over Time)

---

### 🧑‍💻 User Engagement and Conversion
**Insights:**
- Paid users are more active and show better retention than free users.

**Recommendations:**
- Introduce mid-tier plans to ease conversion.
- Offer referral-based trials.
- Launch time-limited promotions to boost paid subscriptions.

**Visual B:** (Activity by User Type)

---

### 🎸 Free User Behavior
**Insights:**
- Rock is the most popular genre among free-tier users.

**Recommendations:**
- Target rock listeners with trial-based promos.
- Launch genre-specific playlists and live events.
- Emphasize indie content for paid user campaigns.

**Visual C:** (Genre Preferences by Subscription Tier)

---

## ✅ Conclusion & Strategic Recommendations

- **Rock for Free Users**: Introduce rock-focused playlists, exclusive events, and artist collaborations to increase engagement.
- **Capitalize on Trending Genres**: Launch electronic and hip hop promotions and playlists to support user growth and artist visibility.
- **Invest in Retention Tactics**: Provide tailored experiences for both free and paid users, including discovery features, time-limited offers, and exclusive content for premium subscribers.
